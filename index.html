How it works

From your switch to every camera on board.

OpenSlate listens to Betaflight, decides when you want to record, and tells your cameras over Bluetooth. Status goes straight back into your OSD.

Your radio

Arm, or flip any AUX switch you like.

ELRS · CRSF · any link

Flight controller

Betaflight reports sticks and arm state, and shows the OSD text.

OpenSlate

Features

Built for the flight, not the bench.

Record on arm

Starts when you arm, stops when you disarm. Or put recording on any AUX switch with your own threshold.

Status in your goggles

REC 85% 02:41, camera battery, link state and camera count on all four Custom Messages.

Two cameras

Copter cam plus stickcam, DJI or GoPro in any mix. One switch, both cameras.

Fast reconnect

A stickcam out of range during the flight comes back within seconds after landing and gets its STOP right away.

Set up from your phone

Join the OpenSlate Wi-Fi and the setup page opens by itself. Pair, choose a switch, done. Works offline.

Flight-safe

Pairing and updates are locked while armed, and Wi-Fi turns off in flight so the radio belongs to your cameras.

Pick what each message shows

Place Custom Message 1–4 anywhere in the Betaflight OSD tab. OpenSlate fills in the text, live.

  1. CAMS 1/2 after landing? Your stickcam is still on its way back.
  2. CAMS 2/2 means both cameras are connected and got their command.
  3. Needs a Betaflight version with Custom Message OSD elements.

FAQ

Before you ask.

Which Betaflight version do I need?

One with Custom Message OSD elements (MSP2_SET_TEXT). Betaflight 4.x and 4.5 do not have them. Recording control itself only needs MSP on a free UART.

Do I need to solder anything to the camera?

No. OpenSlate talks to your camera over Bluetooth, the same way the official phone apps do. Only the ESP32 is wired to the flight controller.

What happens if the stickcam is out of range?

OpenSlate keeps searching quietly in the background and reconnects within seconds when it is back. A STOP you gave while it was away is sent the moment it reconnects. Watch CAMS 2/2 in your OSD.

Will Wi-Fi interfere with my cameras in flight?

By default the OpenSlate hotspot turns off when you arm and comes back when you disarm, so Bluetooth has the radio to itself during the flight.

How do I update later?

Download the update file for your board above and drop it into System → Firmware update in the web app. Or simply flash again from this page.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0: anyone who passes OpenSlate on, also on a device they sell, has to include the complete source code and keep the "OpenSlate by FPVRookie" attribution.
