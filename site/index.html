Your arm switch is your record button.

Free, open-source firmware for a cheap ESP32 board next to your flight controller. Arm, and your copter cam and stickcam start recording together. Disarm, and both stop. Your OSD shows that it worked.

One switch, two cameras, one flight.

The copter cam sits next to OpenSlate. The stickcam stays with your spotter a few metres away and loses the link as soon as you fly off. Here is what you see in the OSD.

  1. On the ground

    Arm

    R2/2 85 91 00:00

    Both cameras start together, without touching either of them. The alert line stays empty.

  2. In the air, 2–8 min

    Stickcam out of range

    R1/2 84 -- 03:12STK LOST

    The copter cam keeps recording. OpenSlate keeps listening for the stickcam at low radio duty.

  3. Landed

    Disarm

    CAM 1/2 82 --STK STOP WAIT

    The copter cam stops at once. The STOP for the stickcam is kept until it is back, and the OSD tells you to leave it on.

  4. Walking back

    Stickcam reconnects

    CAM 2/2 82 90ALL STOPPED

    It gets its STOP right away. On the bench: about 2.4 s from its first signal to ready.

  5. Done

    Power down

    CAM 2/2 82 90

    ALL STOPPED means every camera got its STOP. Switch them off.

Two cameras at once is covered by software tests; the reconnect time was measured on the bench with one camera. There are no flight reports yet. If you fly it, tell us how it went.

Free, because a record button should not cost more than the camera mount.

OpenSlate runs on an off-the-shelf ESP32 board for a few euros. The firmware, the web app and the flasher are open source under GPL-3.0.

You choose what goes where

Place Custom Message 1–4 anywhere in the Betaflight OSD tab and pick their content in the OpenSlate web app. The text updates live.

Two elements are enough. R2/2 85 62 02:41 tells you how many cameras record, their batteries and the time. The alert line stays empty until something needs you, then names the camera: STK NOT REC, STK LOST, ALL STOPPED.

Your Betaflight build needs the Custom Message OSD elements. Betaflight 4.5 and older do not have them. In the OSD tab you will see four entries called Custom Message 1 to 4.

Five steps to your first recording.

  1. Wire the board to your FC

    Four wires to a free UART: 5 V, GND, and TX to RX in both directions.

    ESP32-C3RX GPIO5 ← FC TXTX GPIO6 → FC RX
    ESP32 (4 MB)RX GPIO16 ← FC TXTX GPIO17 → FC RX

  2. Set up Betaflight

    Ports tab: turn on MSP for that UART at 115200. OSD tab: place Custom Message 1–4 where you want them.

  3. Plug the board into your computer

    Use a USB cable that carries data, not a charge-only one. If no port shows up in the next step, hold the board's BOOT button while plugging in.

  4. Click Install OpenSlate

    Pick the port. The page detects whether it is an ESP32-C3 or a classic ESP32 and flashes the matching firmware. Takes one to two minutes.

  5. Join the Wi-Fi and pair

    With your phone, join OpenSlate (password openslate). The setup page opens by itself. Camera tab: Scan, then Pair. Switch tab: pick your switch or turn on record on arm.

Install OpenSlate

Firmware v2.2 for ESP32-C3 and ESP32 (4 MB). Needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer.

This browser cannot talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer.
Flashing only works when this page is opened over https.

Installing erases the board first. On a new board that is what you want. On a board that already runs OpenSlate it also resets your settings and paired cameras.

After installing: unplug the USB cable and power up the quad with the board wired to the FC (steps 1 and 2). Then continue with step 5.

No port shows up

Try a different USB cable: many only charge. Hold BOOT while plugging in. Classic ESP32 boards with a CH340 or CP210x USB chip may need that driver on Windows. ESP32-C3 "Super Mini" boards use the chip's own USB and need no driver.

"Chip not supported"

OpenSlate runs on the ESP32-C3 and the classic ESP32 with 4 MB flash. ESP32-S2, -S3 and -C6 boards are not supported yet.

Flashing stopped halfway

Nothing is broken. Unplug, hold BOOT, plug in again and click Install once more.

Questions before you build.

Which Betaflight version do I need?

Recording control only needs MSP on a free UART, which every Betaflight has. The OSD text needs a build with Custom Message OSD elements: Betaflight 4.5 and older do not have them. If your OSD tab lists Custom Message 1 to 4, you are set.

Do I need to solder anything to the camera?

No. OpenSlate talks to your cameras over Bluetooth. Only the ESP32 is wired to the flight controller.

Which board should I buy?

Any ESP32-C3 board ("Super Mini" or DevKitM-1) or a classic ESP32 DevKitC / WROOM-32 with 4 MB flash. The C3 Super Mini is the smallest and needs no USB driver.

Will Wi-Fi get in the way of my cameras in flight?

By default the OpenSlate hotspot turns off when you arm and comes back a few seconds after you disarm, so Bluetooth has the radio to itself during the flight.

How do I update later?

Two ways. Flash again from this page: quickest, but it resets settings and paired cameras. Or keep everything and update from the OpenSlate web app under System → Firmware update with the update file for your board. The web app refuses a file that does not fit your board.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0: anyone who passes OpenSlate on, including on a device they sell, must include the complete source code and keep the "OpenSlate by FPVRookie" attribution.
